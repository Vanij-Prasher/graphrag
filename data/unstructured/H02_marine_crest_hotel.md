# Marine Crest Hotel

## About
Marine Crest Hotel is a luxury property facing the Arabian Sea on Marine Drive. Its rooftop pool and spa are popular with business travellers, and the Ocean Suites have floor-to-ceiling sea views. It is located in Marine Drive, Mumbai.

## Rooms
Available room types: Deluxe King at ₹14,500 per night for up to 2 guests; Ocean Suite at ₹26,000 per night for up to 3 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 72 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 3 PM and check-out is by 12 PM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Full prepayment is required at the time of booking. Children under 12 stay free; extra beds cost ₹2,500 per night.
