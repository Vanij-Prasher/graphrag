# Panjim Heritage Stay

## About
Panjim Heritage Stay is a restored Portuguese-era home in the colourful Fontainhas Latin Quarter. Rooms have original tiles and wooden balconies, and breakfast is served in the courtyard. It is located in Fontainhas, Panaji, Goa.

## Rooms
Available room types: Heritage Double at ₹4,200 per night for up to 2 guests; Heritage Suite at ₹6,800 per night for up to 3 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 72 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 2 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Not recommended for children under 8 because of steep heritage staircases.
