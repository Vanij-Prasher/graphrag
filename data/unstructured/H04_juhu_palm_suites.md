# Juhu Palm Suites

## About
Juhu Palm Suites offers apartment-style suites with kitchenettes, close to Juhu Beach. It is popular with families and long-stay guests who want more space than a normal hotel room. It is located in Juhu, Mumbai.

## Rooms
Available room types: Studio Suite at ₹7,800 per night for up to 2 guests; Two-Bedroom Suite at ₹13,500 per night for up to 5 guests.

## Policies
All bookings are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled or changed once confirmed. Small dogs under 10 kg are allowed for a fee of ₹800 per night. Check-in starts at 2 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is not included and is charged separately. Full prepayment is required at the time of booking. Children of all ages are welcome.
