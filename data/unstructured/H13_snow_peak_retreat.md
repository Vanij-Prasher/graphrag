# Snow Peak Retreat

## About
Snow Peak Retreat is a wooden mountain lodge in Old Manali with views of snow-capped peaks. Guests gather around the evening bonfire, and the cafe is known for its apple pie. It is located in Old Manali, Manali.

## Rooms
Available room types: Mountain View Room at ₹3,600 per night for up to 2 guests; Attic Family Room at ₹5,400 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are welcome; there is a fenced garden for dogs. Check-in starts at 1 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children are welcome; the attic room suits families.
