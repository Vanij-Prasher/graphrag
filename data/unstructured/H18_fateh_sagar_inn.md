# Fateh Sagar Inn

## About
Fateh Sagar Inn is a mid-range hotel facing Fateh Sagar Lake, with a rooftop restaurant that is busy at sunset. It is located in Fateh Sagar, Udaipur.

## Rooms
Available room types: Lake Facing Double at ₹4,600 per night for up to 2 guests; Triple Room at ₹5,800 per night for up to 3 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 12 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is not included and is charged separately. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children under 5 stay free.
