# Pink City Haveli

## About
Pink City Haveli is an 18th-century haveli converted into a heritage hotel, walking distance from Hawa Mahal and City Palace. The rooftop restaurant has views over the old city. It is located in Old City, Jaipur.

## Rooms
Available room types: Haveli Room at ₹3,900 per night for up to 2 guests; Royal Chamber at ₹6,200 per night for up to 3 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 12 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children are welcome; an extra mattress costs ₹600.
