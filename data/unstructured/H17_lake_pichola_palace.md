# Lake Pichola Palace

## About
Lake Pichola Palace is a heritage luxury hotel on the banks of Lake Pichola, reached by a private boat transfer. It overlooks the City Palace and is one of Udaipur's most famous stays. It is located in Lal Ghat, Udaipur.

## Rooms
Available room types: Lake View Room at ₹18,500 per night for up to 2 guests; Royal Lake Suite at ₹38,000 per night for up to 3 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 96 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 3 PM and check-out is by 12 PM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Full prepayment is required at the time of booking. Children under 12 stay free; a heritage walk for families runs every morning.
