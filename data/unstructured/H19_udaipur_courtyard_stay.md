# Udaipur Courtyard Stay

## About
Udaipur Courtyard Stay is a friendly budget guesthouse in Hanuman Ghat with a rooftop cafe looking across Lake Pichola. It is located in Hanuman Ghat, Udaipur.

## Rooms
Available room types: Courtyard Double at ₹2,100 per night for up to 2 guests; Shared Dorm Bed at ₹700 per night for up to 1 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 12 PM and check-out is by 10 AM. Breakfast is not included and is charged separately. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Dorm beds are for guests aged 18 and over only.
