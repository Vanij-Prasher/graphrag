# Solang Valley Lodge

## About
Solang Valley Lodge is right next to the Solang Valley ski slopes and paragliding points, making it a base for adventure travellers in winter and summer. It is located in Solang, Manali.

## Rooms
Available room types: Valley Room at ₹3,200 per night for up to 2 guests; Group Room at ₹5,900 per night for up to 6 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 12 PM and check-out is by 10 AM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children are welcome; ski gear for kids can be rented on site.
