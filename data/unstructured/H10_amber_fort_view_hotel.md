# Amber Fort View Hotel

## About
Amber Fort View Hotel lies at the foot of Amber Fort in Amer, and many rooms look straight up at the fort walls, which are lit up at night. It is located in Amer, Jaipur.

## Rooms
Available room types: Fort View Double at ₹5,600 per night for up to 2 guests; Family Room at ₹8,100 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Dogs are allowed with prior approval and a refundable ₹2,000 deposit. Check-in starts at 1 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is not included and is charged separately. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children under 6 stay free.
