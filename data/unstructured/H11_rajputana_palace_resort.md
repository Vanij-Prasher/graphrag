# Rajputana Palace Resort

## About
Rajputana Palace Resort is a sprawling luxury resort on the outskirts of Jaipur, built in Rajput palace style with courtyards, fountains and a large spa. It is located in Jagatpura, Jaipur.

## Rooms
Available room types: Palace Room at ₹13,800 per night for up to 2 guests; Maharaja Suite at ₹29,500 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 72 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 2 PM and check-out is by 12 PM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Full prepayment is required at the time of booking. Children under 12 stay free; babysitting is available on request.
