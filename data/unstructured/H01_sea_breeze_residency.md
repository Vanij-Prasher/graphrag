# Sea Breeze Residency

## About
Sea Breeze Residency is a calm boutique hotel tucked into the lanes of Colaba, a short walk from the Gateway of India. Guests often mention the friendly front desk and the quiet rooms despite the busy neighbourhood. It is located in Colaba, Mumbai.

## Rooms
Available room types: Standard Double at ₹6,500 per night for up to 2 guests; Deluxe Sea View at ₹9,200 per night for up to 2 guests; Family Room at ₹11,800 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 2 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children under 6 stay free using existing bedding.
