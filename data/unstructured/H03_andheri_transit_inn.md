# Andheri Transit Inn

## About
Andheri Transit Inn is a practical choice for travellers with early or late flights. It runs a free 24-hour shuttle to Mumbai International Airport, about 1.2 km away. It is located in Andheri East, Mumbai.

## Rooms
Available room types: Standard Twin at ₹3,800 per night for up to 2 guests; Triple Room at ₹4,900 per night for up to 3 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 12 PM and check-out is by 10 AM. Breakfast is not included and is charged separately. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children are welcome; no extra beds are available.
