# Jaipur Budget Stay

## About
Jaipur Budget Stay is a no-frills option near Jaipur Railway Station, offering private rooms and dormitory beds for budget travellers. It is located in Sindhi Camp, Jaipur.

## Rooms
Available room types: Economy Double at ₹1,600 per night for up to 2 guests; Dormitory Bed at ₹650 per night for up to 1 guests.

## Policies
All bookings are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled or changed once confirmed. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 11 AM and check-out is by 10 AM. Breakfast is not included and is charged separately. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult.
