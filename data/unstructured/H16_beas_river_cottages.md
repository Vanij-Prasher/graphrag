# Beas River Cottages

## About
Beas River Cottages are simple riverside cottages in Vashisht village, a short walk from the Vashisht hot springs. It is located in Vashisht, Manali.

## Rooms
Available room types: Riverside Cottage at ₹2,500 per night for up to 2 guests; Family Cottage at ₹3,900 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
All bookings are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled or changed once confirmed. Pets of any size are allowed at no extra charge. Check-in starts at 12 PM and check-out is by 10 AM. Breakfast is not included and is charged separately. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children are welcome.
