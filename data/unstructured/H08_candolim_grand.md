# Candolim Grand

## About
Candolim Grand is a five-star beachfront resort near Fort Aguada, with private pool villas, a full-service spa and direct beach access. It is located in Candolim, Goa.

## Rooms
Available room types: Premier Room at ₹12,500 per night for up to 2 guests; Grand Suite at ₹22,000 per night for up to 3 guests; Pool Villa at ₹34,000 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
All bookings are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled or changed once confirmed. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 3 PM and check-out is by 12 PM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Full prepayment is required at the time of booking. Children under 12 stay free with parents.
