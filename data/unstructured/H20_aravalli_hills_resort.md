# Aravalli Hills Resort

## About
Aravalli Hills Resort is a quiet resort in the Aravalli hills near Badi, about 4 km from the Sajjangarh Monsoon Palace, with a pool, spa and nature trails. It is located in Badi, Udaipur.

## Rooms
Available room types: Hill View Room at ₹7,200 per night for up to 2 guests; Family Suite at ₹11,200 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Dogs are welcome; the resort has walking trails nearby. Check-in starts at 2 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children under 6 stay free and there is a small play lawn.
