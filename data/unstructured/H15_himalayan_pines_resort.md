# Himalayan Pines Resort

## About
Himalayan Pines Resort is set among pine trees a few minutes from Mall Road, with heated rooms, a small spa and cottage suites for families. It is located in Mall Road, Manali.

## Rooms
Available room types: Pine Deluxe at ₹6,900 per night for up to 2 guests; Cottage Suite at ₹10,400 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 72 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Small pets under 10 kg are allowed for ₹500 per night. Check-in starts at 2 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Full prepayment is required at the time of booking. Children under 5 stay free.
