# Palolem Bay Huts

## About
Palolem Bay Huts is a laid-back set of wooden huts directly on Palolem Beach in South Goa. It is a favourite with backpackers and travellers with pets. It is located in Palolem, Goa.

## Rooms
Available room types: Beach Hut at ₹2,200 per night for up to 2 guests; Family Hut at ₹3,400 per night for up to 4 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets of any size are welcome at no extra cost. Check-in starts at 12 PM and check-out is by 10 AM. Breakfast is not included and is charged separately. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Families are welcome; the Family Hut has bunk beds.
