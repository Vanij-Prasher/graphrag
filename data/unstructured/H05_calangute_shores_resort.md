# Calangute Shores Resort

## About
Calangute Shores Resort sits just behind Calangute Beach. The resort has a large lagoon-style pool, a beach shack-style bar and plenty of activities for children. It is located in Calangute, Goa.

## Rooms
Available room types: Garden Room at ₹6,200 per night for up to 2 guests; Pool Access Room at ₹8,900 per night for up to 2 guests; Family Villa at ₹15,500 per night for up to 5 guests.

## Policies
Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in; after that, the first night is charged. Pets are not allowed on the property. Check-in starts at 1 PM and check-out is by 11 AM. Breakfast is included in all room rates. Guests can pay at the hotel on arrival. Children under 5 stay free, and the resort runs a supervised kids club in the afternoons.
